Albus Reacts Publisher

Albus Reacts Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Albus Reacts accounts (the Albus Reacts YouTube channel and TikTok @albus.reacts). It uploads short, edited GTA 6 streamer reaction clips that the owner has reviewed and approved, and reads view counts for our own videos. Every clip credits the original streamer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Uses YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to our own YouTube channel and to read our own videos' statistics. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
