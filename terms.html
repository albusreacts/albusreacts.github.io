Albus Reacts Publisher: Terms of Service

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Albus Reacts Publisher ("the tool") is a private posting tool operated by the owner of the Albus Reacts accounts (TikTok @albus.reacts and the Albus Reacts YouTube channel). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

1. Who can use it. Only the owner of the Albus Reacts accounts uses the tool, and only to publish videos to those accounts. No one else can sign in or post through it.

2. What it does. The tool uploads short, edited GTA 6 streamer reaction clips to the Albus Reacts accounts at scheduled times, up to 3 per day per platform. Every video is reviewed and approved by the account owner before it can be scheduled. Nothing is posted automatically without that approval.

3. Content rules. Every clip credits the original streamer in its caption. Clips are short, edited highlights, never full streams. We do not post leaked or pre-release footage, mods or cheats, or content that breaks the rules of TikTok or YouTube. Paid campaign clips are marked as branded content where required.
