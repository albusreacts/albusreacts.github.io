Albus Reacts Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Albus Reacts Publisher ("the tool") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Albus Reacts accounts (the Albus Reacts YouTube channel and TikTok @albus.reacts) to publish their own approved videos.

1. We use YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

2. What YouTube data we access, and why

  • Our channel's name and ID (youtube.readonly): to confirm the tool is connected to the Albus Reacts channel and no other.
  • Video upload (youtube.upload): to upload the owner's own approved videos to that channel.
  • View, like and comment counts of our own videos (youtube.readonly): to see how our own clips perform.

The tool does not access, collect, store or share data about any other YouTube users, and does not read comments, subscribers or watch history.

3. How it's stored

Access tokens and our videos' statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer, in private files that are never shared. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The website itself uses no cookies and no tracking.

4. How it's refreshed and deleted

YouTube data is refreshed every 6 hours. Any YouTube data that has not been refreshed within 30 days is automatically deleted. When access is revoked or the tool is disconnected, the stored tokens are deleted, and any remaining YouTube data is deleted within 30 days at the latest.

5. How to revoke access

You can revoke the tool's access to your Google account at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. For TikTok: in the TikTok app, Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions.

6. TikTok

For TikTok, the tool uses the same kinds of data (account name and ID, video upload, our own videos' counts) under the same storage and deletion rules.
