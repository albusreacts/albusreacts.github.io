Albus Reacts Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Albus Reacts Publisher ("the tool") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Albus Reacts accounts (TikTok @albus.reacts and the Albus Reacts YouTube channel) to publish their own approved videos.

1. Whose data. The tool only accesses the Albus Reacts accounts, with the owner's permission. It does not access, collect, store or share data about any other TikTok or YouTube users.

2. What it uses, and why.

  • Basic account info (account name and ID): to confirm the tool is connected to the right account.
  • Video upload and publish permissions: to post the owner's own approved videos.
  • Our own videos' view, like and comment counts (read only): to see how our own clips perform.

3. Where it's stored. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private file that is never shared. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. YouTube. The tool uses YouTube API Services. Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can revoke the tool's access at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions

5. TikTok. You can revoke the tool's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions.

6. Keeping and deleting data. Data from YouTube API Services (our own videos' view, like and comment counts) is refreshed every 6 hours. Any YouTube data that has not been refreshed within 30 days is automatically deleted. The owner can disconnect the tool at any time; the stored tokens and YouTube data are then deleted from their computer.

7. Changes. This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
